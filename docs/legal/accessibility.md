## Our goal

We aim for our website and apps to meet the [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/), Level AA. These are the international guidelines for making websites and apps work for people with disabilities, including people who are blind or have low vision, are deaf or hard of hearing, or have motor or cognitive disabilities.

## What we've done so far

### On our website

- **Automated accessibility checks.** Our automated tests check every page of the website, each booking step and the pages of the shop's own tools with axe-core, a widely used accessibility testing tool, at computer and phone screen sizes. Any problem it rates as serious or critical, such as text without enough color contrast or a form field without a label, stops the tests until we fix it.
- **Keyboard.** The website is built to work with a keyboard alone. One of our automated tests books a whole appointment using only the keyboard. A "Skip to main content" link lets keyboard users skip past the menu.
- **Screen readers.** Every page has a title and a main heading. Form fields have labels, and problems with what you've typed are announced. When you go to another page, the focus moves to its main content.
- **Phones.** Our tests check that the main pages, including this one, fit a phone screen 375 pixels wide without scrolling sideways, and that the booking steps and the shop's own pages fit a phone screen 390 pixels wide.
- **Your settings.** The website follows your device's "reduce motion" setting, and it's built to work with the high contrast modes of Windows ("forced colors").

### In our iPhone apps

- **Text size.** The apps' text follows your iPhone's text size setting (Dynamic Type), apart from a few icons, large numbers and short labels, such as "Reviews from Google Maps".
- **VoiceOver.** We've written labels for VoiceOver on buttons, cards and other controls.
- **Reduce Motion.** The Izzy Clipz app follows the iPhone's Reduce Motion setting.

## What we know isn't perfect yet

- **We haven't had people who use assistive technology test our website and apps**, and no outside expert has audited them. Automated checks find only some kinds of problems, so passing them doesn't prove that everything meets WCAG 2.2 Level AA.
- **Our iPhone apps' automated tests don't include accessibility checks yet.**
- **Content from other companies** is outside our control:
  - **Stripe's card page**, where you add a card when a booking needs one, is run by Stripe. It expires after 60 minutes; if that happens, you can start again, or contact us to book.
  - **Reviews from Google** are shown as Google provides them. Reviewers' words and photos come from their authors.
  - **Maps, and the Google, Yelp and Instagram pages** we link to, are run by those companies.
- **Signing in to the shop's own tools on a computer** uses a code that expires after 10 minutes. If it runs out, make a new one in the Izzy Clipz Business app.

## Other ways to book and get help

You never have to use our website or apps to book.

- **Call us at (442) 341-3053** during opening hours. We'll book, change or cancel your appointment for you, tell you about our services and prices, and read you the cancellation terms.
- **Call through a relay service.** If you're deaf, hard of hearing or have a speech disability, call us through any relay service: dial 711, or use video relay, IP relay or a captioned phone as you usually do. We welcome relay calls.
- **Email us at [TO FILL IN: the shop's email address].**
- **Walk in.** Walk-ins are welcome, depending on which barbers are free.
- **At the shop.** If you need help getting in, or any other accommodation during your visit, contact us before you come or tell us when you arrive. Our Visit page lists what we've checked about getting in and around the shop. Service animals are welcome.
