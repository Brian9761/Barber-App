## What we collect, and why

### When you book

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Your name | So we know who's coming. |
| Your US mobile number | So we can reach you about your appointment, and to keep one number from holding too many bookings. It's required when you book online, and optional when you walk in. |
| Your email address (optional, and only asked for on our website) | Only to contact you about your appointment if we need to. |
| A note for your barber (optional, up to 300 characters) | So your barber knows what you'd like. Please don't include health information or anything else sensitive. |
| What you book: the service, the barber, the day and time, and the price shown | To hold your appointment and run the shop's schedule. |

### A card on file, when a booking needs one

When the shop has no-show protection turned on, some bookings need a card on file. Our [Cancellation & No-Show Policy](cancellation-policy.md) explains how it works.

- **You enter your card on Stripe's own secure page**, not ours. Stripe is our payment processor. Stripe's page may also ask for your email address and, depending on your card, your name, country or ZIP code. Stripe keeps these with your card in the shop's Stripe account, where the shop can see them. (So can the developer who runs our systems for us: see "Who we share your information with".)
- **We never see or store your full card number.** We receive and keep the card's type (for example, Visa), its last 4 digits, its expiry month and year, and Stripe's reference numbers for it.
- **We keep a record of what you agreed to:** the exact cancellation and no-show terms you saw before booking, the fee, how long before the appointment cancelling stopped being free, and the time you booked. It's the record of your agreement, and you can ask us for a copy.

### What happens to your appointments

We keep what happens to each appointment: whether it was completed, cancelled (when, and by whom) or missed, and whether a fee was charged. We use this to run the shop, to keep its records and to apply the Cancellation & No-Show Policy.

The shop's sales reports use services, prices and totals. They don't use your name, number, email or note.

### At the shop

Our staff use Izzy Clipz Business to:

- **book walk-ins and phone bookings**, with your name and, if you give them, your mobile number and a note;
- **check you out**, which records what you had and how you paid. The record can include your name.
- **take card payments with Tap to Pay on iPhone.** Your card is read by Apple's Tap to Pay technology and processed by Stripe, so our apps and server never see your card number. Stripe receives the amount and a short description of your visit, such as the service and the barber, or a note our staff type.
- **send a receipt by email, if you'd like one.** Our staff offer it only after your card payment has gone through, and you never need to give an email address to pay by card. We pass the address to Stripe, which emails you the receipt, and we don't keep it.

### Technical information

- **IP addresses.** To stop abuse, such as too many booking attempts from one network, our server keeps a scrambled form of your IP address for about 2 days. We don't store your IP address anywhere else.
- **Hosting logs.** Our hosting provider, Vercel, processes your IP address and basic details of each request (such as the page requested, your browser type and the time) to deliver our website and apps. The logs we can see are kept for up to 30 days. Vercel may keep some traffic information, such as IP addresses, for longer, under its own Privacy Notice.
- **No analytics, ads or tracking.** We don't use analytics, advertising, social media pixels or crash-reporting tools on our website or in our apps, and we don't build profiles of you. (The card payment software from Stripe in the staff app reports to Stripe; see "The shop's own tools".)
- **What our website loads, and from where.** Our website's pages, fonts and pictures come from our own hosting provider. The only other companies it loads anything from are Google, for reviewers' photos (see below), and Stripe, only when you go to Stripe's page to add a card. The Izzy Clipz app also loads map pictures from Apple to show where the shop is, without using your location.

### Reviews from Google

Our website and the Izzy Clipz app show reviews of the shop from Google, as Google provides them. We also link to the shop's reviews on Yelp, but we don't show them on our website or in our app.

- **If you're viewing reviews:** reviewers' profile photos load directly from Google's servers, so Google receives your device's IP address and browser details, as it would for any picture.
- **If you've reviewed the shop on Google:** your public review, with your name and photo as they appear on Google, may show on our website and in our app. We don't store reviews from Google. To change or remove your review, do it on Google.

### Photos

We show a photo in which someone can be recognized, such as a photo of a haircut done at the shop, only if that person agreed to be shown (for anyone under 18, a parent or guardian agreed). If you're in a photo on our website or in our app and want it taken down, contact us. We'll remove it from our website as soon as we can, and from the app in its next update.

## Cookies and storage on your device

### On our website

We use **one cookie**, called `izzy_appointments`. It's a first-party, functional cookie. It carries the appointments you booked in this browser (each one's ID and its key to manage it) into the app you add to your Home Screen, which keeps its own storage. Your browser sends it to our server with each request, but our server doesn't read it. It ends 60 days after it was last updated, each appointment in it is dropped 30 days after the appointment, and the demo never sets it.

We don't use advertising or analytics cookies. Stripe may use cookies on its own card page.

Our website also keeps a few things in your browser's storage, for your convenience:

| What | When it's removed |
|---|---|
| The appointments you booked in this browser, with their keys (never your name, number, email or note) | 30 days after each appointment, or when you choose "Forget me" |
| Your name, number and email, only if you turn on "Remember me" (it's off unless this browser already remembers you) | When you choose "Forget me" or "Not you?" |
| The key to your saved card, with its type, last 4 digits and expiry, and the number of the person who added it | With "Remember me", when you choose "Forget me"; without it, after you book or when you close the tab |
| The booking you're in the middle of, including your details (and, while you add a card, what's needed to come back from Stripe's page) | When you book, close the tab or choose "Forget me" |
| The last appointment you booked in this tab, so its confirmation can be shown again | When you close the tab or choose "Forget me" |
| Whether the "Add to Home Screen" bar was closed, and a count of visits for that bar | When you clear your browser's data for our website |
| For shop staff only: the computer's sign-in to Izzy Clipz Business (never the owner key), and the barber last picked at checkout | The sign-in: when staff sign out, or after 30 days |

To work offline, our website also saves copies of its own pages, pictures and the shop's public menu on your device. It never saves your personal information there.

### In the Izzy Clipz iPhone app

- **"Remember me on this iPhone"** is off unless you turn it on, or this iPhone already remembers you from an earlier booking. When it's on, the app keeps your name and number on this iPhone for your next booking and, if you add a card, the key to that card, so you can use it again.
- **Your appointments.** The app keeps the appointments you booked on this iPhone, including your name, number and note and the card's type and last 4 digits, until 30 days after each appointment.
- **Keys.** It keeps the key to manage each appointment in the iPhone's Keychain. These keys can move to a new iPhone with an encrypted backup. A booking that's still being sent is kept there too, with your number, until the shop's server confirms it or its time has passed. The key to your saved card stays on this iPhone only and isn't included in backups.
- **Preferences.** It remembers your favorite barber and your reminder settings, including the date of your last haircut if you use haircut reminders.
- **Permissions.** The app doesn't ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. After you book, it may ask to send notifications, for that appointment's reminders: the day before, an hour before and, if cancelling late can be charged, before free cancelling ends. These reminders are on unless you turn them off on "You're booked". The app also asks when you turn on haircut reminders. "Add to Calendar" uses the iPhone's own calendar screen, so the app can't read your calendar.
- **Clearing it.** "Forget my details" removes your remembered name and number, and your saved card, from this iPhone. Your appointments stay in the app, with your name, number and note, until 30 days after each one; "Remove from this list" takes a past or cancelled appointment off sooner, together with its key. Deleting the app deletes its list of appointments, but the iPhone's Keychain can keep the keys to your appointments and your saved card, and any booking still being sent. So before you delete the app, use "Forget my details" and remove your past appointments from the list.

### Saved cards

A card you save on Stripe's page stays in the shop's Stripe account until the first of these happens:

- the card expires;
- 12 months pass without it being used for a booking;
- you remove it ("Remove card" or "Forget my details" in the app, or "Forget me" on our website), or ask us to delete it;
- you saved it without "Remember me", and the booking you saved it for can no longer be charged a fee (7 days after the appointment at the latest).

With "Remember me", that's so you can book again quickly. Without it, the card is used only for the booking you saved it for.

Then we ask Stripe to delete the card, and the customer record it was saved under, as soon as none of your bookings still needs it (an upcoming appointment, or one that can still be charged a fee). After that, the card can't be charged again. The shop's Stripe account still keeps a record of the card page you filled in, including the email address you typed there, and of any fee charged, and Stripe keeps its own records, as its policies and the law require.

We may keep information longer when the law requires it, or when we need it to resolve a dispute or a legal claim.

## How we protect it

- **Card numbers** are handled only by Stripe and Apple, never by us. Card payments are processed by Stripe, which is certified to the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS).
- **Connections** to our website, apps, server and database are encrypted.
- **Keys and sessions.** The keys that let you manage an appointment or use a saved card, and the shop's computer sign-ins, are stored on our server only in scrambled (hashed) form.
- **Shop access.** Izzy Clipz Business is protected by an owner key and one-time sign-in codes, and only the shop's staff use it. The developer who runs our systems for us also has access, as described in "Who we share your information with".
- **Limits and logs.** We limit how many booking and card requests can come from one network, and our server's own logs are designed not to record your name, number, email or what you type.
- **Less data, kept for less time**, as described above.

No system is perfectly secure, so we can't promise that information will never be accessed without permission.

## Your choices and rights

California law gives California residents rights over their personal information. Whether or not a particular law applies to a small business like ours, we offer these rights to everyone:

- **Know and get a copy.** You can ask what personal information we have about you, and get a copy, including the cancellation and no-show terms you agreed to.
- **Correct it.** You can ask us to correct information that's wrong. For an upcoming appointment, we can also cancel it and book it again with the right details, with no fee.
- **Delete it.** You can ask us to delete your personal information. We'll remove your name, number, email and note from our records and delete your saved card, except for what we still need: for example, an appointment that's coming up or can still be charged a fee, or records the law requires us to keep.
- **No selling or sharing.** We don't sell your personal information or share it for advertising, so there's nothing you need to opt out of.
- **Fair treatment.** We won't treat you differently for using any of these rights.

**How to ask.** Call us at (442) 341-3053, email us at [TO FILL IN: the shop's email address], visit the shop, or write to us at the address below. Someone else can make a request for you if you give them written permission.

**How we check it's you.** We don't have accounts, so we'll ask for your name, your mobile number and a detail of one of your appointments, such as its date. Then we'll call or text the number we have on file to confirm. We use what you give us only to check and answer your request.

**When we'll answer.** We'll confirm we've received your request within 10 business days, and answer it within 45 days. If we need more time, we'll tell you why and take up to 45 more days.

**Choices on your own device.** You can turn off "Remember me", use "Forget me" (website) or "Forget my details" (app), remove a saved card, turn off reminders, and clear your browser's data for our website at any time.

## The shop's own tools

Izzy Clipz Business is for the shop's staff. Here's what it handles about them:

- **The owner key** is kept on the shop's server and in the Keychain of the shop's iPhone, on that iPhone only. It's never stored on a computer: computers sign in with a one-time code instead.
- **Signed-in computers.** For each computer signed in to Izzy Clipz Business, our server keeps a name made from its browser (for example, "Chrome on Mac") and when it was last used. Each sign-in lasts at most 30 days, and ended sign-ins are deleted when the next computer signs in.
- **Product photos.** The app uses the camera and photo library only for photos of the shop's products. Before a photo is uploaded, it's redrawn on the device, which removes its location and other camera details. Product photos are public, because the client app shows them.
- **Tap to Pay on iPhone.** Stripe needs to know where card payments are taken, to reduce fraud and disputes, so its software checks the iPhone's location while a payment is taken; without it, Tap to Pay is turned off. Stripe's software also sends Stripe the iPhone's approximate location and information about how its payment features are used. The app declares Bluetooth only because Stripe's software supports Bluetooth card readers; it doesn't connect to Bluetooth devices.
- **Sales by barber.** The sales credited to each barber can be seen by everyone who uses Izzy Clipz Business.
